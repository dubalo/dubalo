Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NeuroTrack ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize your privacy. Symptom data entered in our tracking tools is stored locally on your device using localStorage. We do not store this medical information on our servers unless specifically requested for backup services in the future.

2. Google Analytics and AdSense

We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic and Google AdSense to serve advertisements. These services may use cookies to collect non-identifiable information to improve user experience.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Since your symptom log is stored locally, its security also depends on your device's security.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.
