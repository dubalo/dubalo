Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Senior Health Care ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal identifiers unless explicitly provided by you (e.g., through our symptom log which uses localStorage or optional form submissions). The symptom data is stored locally on your device unless you choose to submit it via our contact forms.

2. Google Analytics and AdSense

We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic and Google AdSense to serve advertisements. These services may use cookies and web beacons to collect information as you interact with our site. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
