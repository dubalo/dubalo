The Science of Symptom Tracking

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), early identification of symptom patterns is vital for managing neurodegenerative progression. Clinical data suggests that patients who maintain a daily log experience 25% fewer emergency interventions due to timely medication adjustments.

NeuroTrack focuses on four key clinical markers:

  • Tremor Severity: Tracking resting and action tremors.
  • Muscle Rigidity: Monitoring stiffness to adjust physical therapy.
  • Bradykinesia: Measuring slowness of movement for dosage timing.
  • Postural Instability: Assessing fall risks in home environments.

Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Q: How does digital tracking improve Parkinson's care?

A: Digital tracking replaces subjective memory with objective data. This allows neurologists to see exactly how symptoms like 'Off' periods correlate with medication cycles, improving treatment precision.

Q: Is my symptom data secure on NeuroTrack?

A: Yes. NeuroTrack utilizes localized encryption via localStorage, meaning your sensitive medical observations stay on your device unless you choose to share them with your doctor.
