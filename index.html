Managing Parkinson's and Dementia

Caring for a loved one with Parkinson’s or dementia requires a holistic approach that combines medical treatment, physical activity, and mental support. Understanding the daily fluctuations in symptoms can help caregivers and doctors make informed decisions about care plans.

Key areas of focus for daily care:

  • Consistent Routine: Maintaining a regular schedule for meals, exercise, and sleep can reduce anxiety and confusion.
  • Physical Activity: Light exercises, such as walking or stretching, help maintain mobility and balance.
  • Effective Communication: Use simple, direct sentences and maintain a calm tone to improve understanding.
  • Safety First: Ensure the living environment is free of hazards to prevent falls and accidents.

The Importance of Symptom Tracking

Neurodegenerative diseases often progress slowly and symptoms can vary significantly from day to day. By using our Symptom Log, you can capture these nuances and provide a comprehensive data set to your medical team. This helps in identifying patterns, such as the effectiveness of certain medications at specific times of the day.

📌 Important Daily Reminders

• Never skip prescribed medication without consulting a doctor.
• Encourage social interaction to maintain cognitive health.
• Monitor hydration and nutrition carefully.
• Take time for your own self-care as a caregiver.
