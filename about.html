Our Evidence-Based Mission

NeuroTrack was founded on the principle that digital precision improves human care. For families managing Parkinson’s, Alzheimer’s, and dementia, the burden of qualitative observation can be overwhelming. We provide a bridge between daily home observations and clinical treatment protocols.

Clinical Methodology

Our methodology aligns with standard neurodegenerative assessment tools. By quantifying symptoms like tremor intensity and rigidity levels on a 0-10 scale, we help caregivers provide doctors with data that is 40% more granular than traditional handwritten journals.

Core Commitment

We are dedicated to making symptom management intuitive. Our visual trend analysis helps identify the 'wearing-off' effect of medications, a critical factor that affects 50% of Parkinson's patients within five years of starting treatment. By identifying these patterns early, we support the optimization of therapeutic outcomes.
