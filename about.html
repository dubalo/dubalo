About Senior Health Care

Our mission is to provide support for families and caregivers of seniors living with conditions such as Parkinson’s, Alzheimer’s, and dementia. We believe that tracking symptoms and maintaining a consistent health record is a key part of specialized care.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated group of care support professionals and technology experts focused on making daily symptom management simpler for caregivers. Our tools are designed for clarity and ease of use, ensuring that you can focus more on the human connection with your loved ones.

Why We Do It

Managing neurodegenerative diseases is a marathon, not a sprint. By providing visual trends of symptoms, we help caregivers communicate more effectively with medical professionals, leading to better-tailored treatment plans and improved quality of life.
