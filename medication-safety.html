⚠️ Critical Medication Safety Tips

  • Timing is Key: For Parkinson's, taking Levodopa at the exact same time every day is crucial for avoiding "Off" periods.
  • Protein Interaction: High-protein meals can interfere with Levodopa absorption. Try taking it 30-60 minutes before or after meals.
  • Hydration: Always take medications with a full glass of water unless directed otherwise.
  • Never Skip: If you miss a dose, do not double up. Consult your doctor or pharmacist immediately.
